# Alpinizmo terminų žodynas · English – Lithuanian

The Lithuanian words to use for mountaineering, climbing and ski-touring terms in reports and any other
text on alpinism. It exists because translating an English sentence word by word gives Lithuanian
that no climber would write ("ledo kirvis", "kramponai", "virvės komanda"). Look a term up here before
you write it; do not translate it yourself.

Entries marked **?** are the least certain.

## How to read it

- **Write**: the form to use in written text. Where several are given, the first is the default.
- **Also / avoid**:
  - *žod.*: the form the 2020 alpinism dictionary prefers where it differs from what climbers write.
    Not wrong, but not the default here.
  - *šnek.*: climbers' slang. Use it only in a quote or a deliberately informal diary line.
  - *ntk.*: do not use it: the dictionary marks it as not recommended, or it is a literal translation.
- **Sources**: Ž = the 2020 alpinism dictionary, V = VLE (Visuotinė lietuvių enciklopedija),
  T = Terminų bankas (VLKK), G = Lithuanian climbing reports, P = the most common usage as far as
  could be told, with no written source found. A row without a source is the usage of Lithuanian
  climbing reports.
- **?**: unsure. Check before you rely on it, and do not build a sentence around it.

Names are never translated: Col du Midi, Adlerpass and Glacier du Géant stay as they are, even though
*col*, *Pass* and *glacier* have Lithuanian entries below.
